How it works

A simple and efficient workflow to bring your vision to life. From the first call to final delivery, every step is designed for clarity and efficiency.

1

Book a Call

Let’s start with a conversation! We’ll discuss your goals, ideas, and how I can help bring them to life. This is where we align expectations and ensure a great fit.

2

Project Brief

Once we’re on the same page, we’ll define the project scope, timeline, and deliverables. A structured brief ensures a smooth and efficient process.

3

Develop

This is where the magic happens! I’ll craft thoughtful designs and refine them based on feedback, ensuring the final outcome meets your vision and goals.

4

Deliver

The final product is polished and ready to go. Whether it’s a brand identity, UI/UX design, or a Framer website, you’ll receive all the necessary files and guidance for a seamless handoff.

5

Support

Enjoy one month of free support after project delivery. I’ll be available to answer questions, make minor adjustments, and ensure everything runs smoothly.

What’s your typical project timeline?

The timeline depends on the project’s scope and complexity. Branding and UI/UX projects usually take 2–6 weeks, while Framer website development can range from 1–4 weeks. I’ll provide a detailed timeline after the project brief.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, revisions are included in the process to ensure you are fully satisfied with the final result. Specific terms are outlined in the project brief.

What do you need from me to get started?

A clear understanding of your goals, any existing brand materials, and your timeline. We'll discuss the specifics on our kickoff call.

Can you work with my existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. I can adapt and build upon your current brand identity to ensure a consistent and elevated look.

Do you provide post-project support?

Yes, every project includes one month of free support for minor adjustments and questions.

What’s the best way to reach you?

You can email me directly or book a call using the links provided on this site.
